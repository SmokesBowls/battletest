# Blood Echo

When the **Rune of the Crimson Bloom** fuses with the bearer’s essence, a dormant function awakens:  
**Blood Echo**.

With this passive resonance, Geralt can touch dried blood, wounded earth, or flesh remnants and receive **visions**—brief flashes of **emotion**, **ancestral trauma**, or **stored vril signatures**.

This allows players to:
- Reveal unseen events tied to a location
- Sense deception (if blood speaks a different truth)
- Access ancestral knowledge or memories from NPCs or enemies
- Detect vril patterns in hybrid constructs or corrupted zones

Blood Echo can also be used in ritual events, unlocking deeper Nefereti trials or Aeon Keeper pathways that require lineage verification.

The deeper the wound, the louder the echo.

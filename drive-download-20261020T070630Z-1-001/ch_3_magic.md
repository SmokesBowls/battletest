#### **Magic & Powers**  
- **Keen**  
  - *Shadow Autonomy*: Freezes reptilians mid-motion using shadow chains.  
  - *Phase Detection*: Senses threats through shadows; implied ability to "see too much."  

- **Tran**  
  - *Crimson Blood Resonance*: Blood pulses in sync with the Star Needle; enhances combat reflexes.  
  - *Needle Harmony*: Subconsciously channels ancient power through breath and pulse.  

- **Rongtai**  
  - *Shadow Entities*: Creates sluggish time fields and shadow chains to immobilize enemies.  

- **Protagonist**  
  - *Blood Resonance*: Awakened power from Star Needle; manifests as a blood whip (temporarily lost).  
  - *Akashic Bond*: Blood binds to the Akashic Records, preserving memories in the Library.  

- **The Mikas**  
  - *Reality Tearing*: Opens portals to the Akashic Records.  
  - *Chronological Preservation*: Anchors travelers’ stories in the Library’s timeless archives.  

- **Star Needle**  
  - *Ancient Pulse*: Glows in sync with the protagonist’s blood; enables healing and power surges.

#### **Key Notes**  
- Magic in Chapter 2 is raw and unpredictable (e.g., Keen’s shadows falter, protagonist’s power flickers).  
- The Akashic Records serve as a narrative anchor, storing progress and offering cryptic guidance.  
- Shadow magic (Keen, Rongtai) and blood-based abilities (Tran, protagonist) dominate the chapter.

Here’s the **refined spell set** for future use, polished with the correct **element alignment** and **effect percentages** while preserving the theme of sacrifice, life, and death. These spells will remain versatile for any character, not just tied to Thang:

---

### **Core Spells (3-5)**

#### **Sanguine Bond**  
- **Effect**: Links the caster’s life force with an ally, healing them for **25% of the caster’s max HP** over 3 turns. The caster loses 15% of their current HP per turn.  
- **Element**: Spirit  
- **Visual/Auditory Cue**: A crimson tether connects the caster and ally, pulsing rhythmically with a soft heartbeat sound.  

---

#### **Ashen Revival**  
- **Effect**: Revives a fallen ally with **50% of their max HP**, but the caster suffers a **10% defense reduction** for 2 turns.  
- **Element**: Spirit + Fire  
- **Visual/Auditory Cue**: Grey ashes swirl and form into the ally’s silhouette, reigniting with a burst of flames.  

---

#### **Blood Infusion**  
- **Effect**: Channels the caster’s blood to empower an ally, granting them **+20% attack power** or **+15% defense** for 3 turns. Costs 10% of the caster’s current HP.  
- **Element**: Spirit + Earth  
- **Visual/Auditory Cue**: The ally glows with a red aura, with a faint dripping sound as energy flows into them.  

---

### **Supportive/Utility Spells (2-4)**

#### **Veil of Shadows**  
- **Effect**: Creates a shadow duplicate of an ally that absorbs **50% of incoming damage** for 1 turn, confusing enemies and redirecting their attacks.  
- **Element**: Shadow  
- **Visual/Auditory Cue**: A dark mist envelops the ally, forming a translucent clone with a low, eerie hum.  

---

#### **Blood Shield**  
- **Effect**: Absorbs **30% of incoming damage** for an ally, with the caster losing HP equal to 15% of the absorbed damage. Lasts 2 turns.  
- **Element**: Spirit + Shadow  
- **Visual/Auditory Cue**: A glowing red shield forms around the ally, crackling with energy as it absorbs hits.  

---

### **Ultimate/Power Spells (1-2)**

#### **Fiery Rebirth**  
- **Effect**: The caster sacrifices themselves to deal **150% AoE Fire damage** to enemies and revive all fallen allies with **25% HP**. Costs 50% of the caster’s max HP or their life.  
- **Element**: Fire + Spirit  
- **Visual/Auditory Cue**: Flames consume the caster, erupting into a radiant phoenix-like burst that revives allies.  

---

### **Situational Spells (1-3)**

#### **Bloodfire Barrier**  
- **Effect**: Summons a barrier that protects allies, reducing incoming damage by **20%**, while dealing **10% Fire DoT** to nearby shadow creatures for 3 turns.  
- **Element**: Fire + Shadow  
- **Visual/Auditory Cue**: A fiery, blood-red wall forms around allies, crackling with flames and emitting deep, echoing roars.

### **Lore and Theme**
- **Sacrifice**: Every ability revolves around giving a piece of the caster to empower, protect, or heal allies, embodying the essence of sacrifice.  
- **Life and Death**: Themes of resurrection and temporary empowerment align with the delicate balance between vitality and loss.  
- **Gothic Feel**: The dark, gothic aesthetic adds depth and ties the spells to a lore-heavy, morally complex background.

---

### **Mechanical Balance**
1. **High Risk, High Reward**:  
   - Spells like **Fiery Rebirth** and **Blood Shield** come with significant costs but provide immense strategic value.  
2. **Cooldowns**:  
   - Powerful spells like **Ashen Revival** and **Fiery Rebirth** should have long cooldowns or steep costs to balance their potency.  

---

### **Player Choice and Progression**
- **Morality and Strategy**: Players must make tough decisions about when to sacrifice resources or health for the team.  
- **Unlockable Potential**: Advanced versions of these spells could include synergy effects, such as **Fiery Rebirth** amplifying the revived allies' abilities temporarily.

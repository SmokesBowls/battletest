Expanded Elemental Combinations with Level Progressions

To keep consistency, I'll break down each combination into its Level 1, 2, and 3 versions, including cooperative effects, progression benefits, AoE warnings, and field interaction details. 1. Water + Fire: Steam Effect

    Primary Elements: Water first, followed by Fire.
    Progression Levels:

        Level 1: Steam Cloud
            Effect: Deals 20 DoT (Damage over Time) for 2 turns. Applies a Light Blind debuff (reduces accuracy by 10%) on enemies within the AoE radius.
            AoE: Affects a 1-pad radius around the target.
            Co-op Attack Bonus: Pairing Water Burst and Flame Burst adds a +5% damage boost on all Steam effects.
            Risk: Allies caught in the AoE without the Mist Veil skill suffer a 10% movement speed reduction.

        Level 2: Heavy Steam Cloud
            Effect: DoT increased to 35 for 3 turns. Now applies a Moderate Blind (reduces accuracy by 20%) and increases fire damage to the target by 15%.
            AoE: Affects a 2-pad radius.
            Landing Benefit: Players staying in the steam area receive Steam Resistance, reducing fire-based damage by 10% for the next 3 turns.

        Level 3: Scalding Mist
            Effect: DoT increased to 50 for 4 turns. Deals additional Burn damage of 25 if the target has an existing fire status effect. Blinds affected enemies by 35%.
            AoE: Covers 3-pads in a wide radius.
            Co-op Effect: When two players stay in the radius and attack, they generate Enhanced Steam Cover, boosting their critical hit chance by 15%.
            Risk: Allies caught in the AoE require Aegis Ward or take 15 damage each turn.

2. Fire + Lightning: Plasma Impact

    Primary Elements: Fire first, followed by Lightning.
    Progression Levels:

        Level 1: Plasma Spark
            Effect: Basic single-target damage of 50. Generates Static Charge, increasing caster’s speed by 10% for 1 turn.
            AoE: Only direct target affected.
            Landing Pad Effect: Landing on the pad generates Static Conduction, reducing MP costs of lightning abilities by 5%.

        Level 2: Plasma Arc
            Effect: Increases to 70 single-target damage. Static Charge increases caster’s speed by 20% for 2 turns.
            AoE: Deals splash damage to adjacent pads (30 damage).
            Field Impact: Electrically charges adjacent pads, increasing lightning attack effectiveness by 25% in those zones.

        Level 3: Plasma Shockwave
            Effect: Deals 100 primary damage, plus an additional 25 to any enemy previously affected by a Fire status. Increases speed by 35% for 3 turns.
            AoE: Full coverage of 3-pads with electric arcs chaining to adjacent enemies.
            Co-op Effect: Players landing within charged pads gain an additional Electric Surge, boosting lightning-based attacks by 15% for the next 2 turns.

3. Earth + Ice: Crystal Explosion

    Primary Elements: Earth first, followed by Ice.
    Progression Levels:

        Level 1: Crystal Crack
            Effect: Deals 40 AoE damage to nearby enemies. Chance of Freeze (20%) if the target is on an Earth-altered terrain.
            AoE: Affects 1-pad radius.
            Landing Bonus: Players on Crystal Shards terrain take 5 less damage from ice abilities.

        Level 2: Crystal Shard Explosion
            Effect: Deals 60 AoE damage. Targets have a 35% chance of being Frozen. If frozen, they take an additional 15 DoT for 2 turns.
            AoE: Covers a 2-pad radius.
            Field Effect: Leaves behind Frozen Shard Terrain, making Ice attacks stronger but reducing Earth attacks' effectiveness in that area.

        Level 3: Crystalline Catastrophe
            Effect: Deals 90 AoE damage, with a 50% freeze chance. Explodes frozen enemies for an additional 30 splash damage.
            AoE: Covers 3-pads and extends damage to adjacent areas.
            Co-op Effect: Players landing within the explosion radius are unaffected if they have Crystal Armor, which also boosts their Ice attacks by 25%.

4. Spirit + Moon: Ethereal Form

    Primary Elements: Spirit first, followed by Moon.
    Progression Levels:

        Level 1: Ethereal Shroud
            Effect: Makes the target untargetable for 1 turn, and increases movement by 20%.
            Co-op Effect: Players in Ethereal Form can link up and move past enemies without triggering counterattacks.

        Level 2: Ethereal Phase
            Effect: Untargetable for 2 turns. Increased attack by 15% during this phase.
            Landing Bonus: Landing on an enemy pad while ethereal creates a Phase Anchor, reducing enemy defense by 20% in that zone.

        Level 3: Ethereal Manifestation
            Effect: Untargetable for 3 turns, can pass through enemies, and boosts attack by 25%.
            Co-op Attack: Players who land together on adjacent pads can execute a Celestial Strike, dealing bonus spirit damage and knocking enemies back into their own column.

5. Shadow + Lightning: Shadow Storm

    Primary Elements: Shadow first, followed by Lightning.
    Progression Levels:

        Level 1: Shadow Flash
            Effect: Deals 35 damage to the enemy and generates a Dark Charge, giving a 5% chance of paralyzing the enemy for 1 turn.
            AoE: Hits the primary target.
            Landing Pad Effect: Pads affected by Shadow Flash generate a Static Veil, where players take 5 less lightning damage.

        Level 2: Shadow Surge
            Effect: Deals 60 damage and boosts the paralysis chance to 20% for enemies under a shadow effect.
            AoE: Strikes the target and arcs to two additional pads (30 damage).
            Field Impact: Creates Veiled Ground, increasing Shadow-based damage by 20%.

        Level 3: Shadow Thunderstorm
            Effect: Deals 90 damage, applies Dark Charge, and increases paralysis chance to 35% for 2 turns.
            AoE: Affects the entire enemy column.
            Co-op Effect: When a player in the Veiled Ground casts a lightning spell, it generates Empowered Darkness, where all enemies in the affected area take an additional 20 DoT for 2 turns.

Learning and Mastery in the Library:

    Learning Phases:
        Basic Elements: Players can access basic elemental spells upon character progression or through guided quests.
        Combination Scrolls: Found throughout the Library of Aeon Keepers, these scrolls describe the potential elemental combinations and their Level 1 effects.
        Advanced Scrolls: Only unlocked after the player has successfully used the Level 1 version in combat at least 5 times.
        Mastery Trials: To gain Level 3 abilities, players need to:
            Complete a Mastery Quest, which involves using cooperative attacks in timed trials.
            Defeat a Guardian of Knowledge—a spectral protector of elemental lore who tests your knowledge of cooperative strategies.

    Library Exploration:
        The library is filled with lore tablets that provide hints and riddles about hidden combinations.
        Players can also train with NPC Aeon Keepers who specialize in specific elemental types, offering challenges to help refine cooperative tactics.
        Unlocking Synergy: Finding a special Synergy Crystal in the library allows players to enhance their elemental combos, granting secondary benefits like reduced MP costs or extra turns for AoE effects.

Kiến Thi Thuý Anh: Dual Path Magic System
Earth Maiden Path
Core Spells

    Earth’s Embrace
        Effect: Restores 15% of max HP to allies in a small radius, scaling with her magic power.
        Element: Earth
        Visual/Auditory Cue: A glowing green aura spreads outward, with soft rumbling sounds of shifting soil.

    Stone Wall
        Effect: Summons a barrier that absorbs damage equal to 25% of her max HP for 2 turns.
        Element: Earth
        Visual/Auditory Cue: Large rocks rise from the ground, accompanied by a deep, resonant vibration.

    Root Bind
        Effect: Immobilizes enemies for 2 turns and reduces their evasion by 10%.
        Element: Earth
        Visual/Auditory Cue: Thick roots burst from the ground, entangling enemies with creaking sounds.

Unique Moon Spell Retained

    Moon’s Blessing
        Effect: Boosts agility and attack speed by 15% for 3 turns.
        Element: Moon
        Visual/Auditory Cue: A soft silver glow envelops allies, with a faint celestial chime.

Situational Spell

    Nature’s Wrath
        Effect: Summons a swarm of insects to deal 10% DoT for 3 turns and distract enemies, reducing their accuracy by 10%.
        Element: Earth
        Visual/Auditory Cue: A buzzing cloud of insects engulfs enemies, with chaotic buzzing sounds.

Supportive/Utility Spell

    Fertile Ground
        Effect: Heals 5% HP per turn for 3 turns for herself and nearby allies.
        Element: Earth
        Visual/Auditory Cue: A lush, green aura grows underfoot, with sounds of grass rustling.

Ultimate Spell

    Gaia’s Awakening
        Effect: Summons a giant earth elemental with 50% of her max HP and 20% of her attack power, lasting 3 turns.
        Element: Earth
        Visual/Auditory Cue: A towering elemental rises from the ground, accompanied by a booming roar.

Moon Queen Path
Core Spells

    Lunar Eclipse
        Effect: AoE damage scaling with moon phases: New Moon (75%), Half Moon (100%), Full Moon (125%).
        Element: Moon
        Visual/Auditory Cue: A dark aura fills the battlefield, with a haunting hum as enemies are engulfed in silver light.

    Tide of Shadows
        Effect: Debuffs enemies by reducing their attack power and accuracy by 15% for 2 turns.
        Element: Shadow + Moon
        Visual/Auditory Cue: Dark waves ripple through the air, with whispers of shadows.

    Celestial Guidance
        Effect: Enhances allies’ attacks, granting +20% magic power and a 10% chance to blind enemies for 2 turns.
        Element: Moon
        Visual/Auditory Cue: A radiant silver light shines from above, illuminating allies.

Unique Earth Spell Retained

    Earth’s Fury
        Effect: Summons a massive earthquake, dealing 100% Earth damage and stunning enemies for 1 turn.
        Element: Earth
        Visual/Auditory Cue: The ground shatters with thunderous cracks, sending tremors through the battlefield.

Situational Spell

    Starfall
        Effect: Rains meteorites, dealing 75% AoE Moon damage with a 25% chance to burn enemies.
        Element: Moon + Fire
        Visual/Auditory Cue: Streaks of silver and fiery meteors crash down, accompanied by loud explosions.

Supportive/Utility Spell

    Lunar Shield
        Effect: Creates a protective aura reflecting 50% of magic damage back to attackers for 2 turns.
        Element: Moon
        Visual/Auditory Cue: A shimmering silver shield surrounds allies, with a gentle hum of celestial energy.

Ultimate Spell

    Cosmic Convergence
        Effect: Calls down a massive moonbeam, dealing 150% AoE Moon damage and blinding enemies for 1 turn.
        Element: Moon
        Visual/Auditory Cue: A bright, blinding light descends, accompanied by an ethereal, resonant chime.

Spiritual Warrior Aspect
Spiritual Strike

    Effect: A melee attack infused with elemental energy, dealing an additional 25% damage based on her current path’s element (Earth or Moon).
    Element: Earth (Earth Maiden) or Moon (Moon Queen)
    Visual/Auditory Cue: A surge of elemental energy trails her weapon, with a sharp crack or shimmer based on the element.

Additional Considerations

    Passive Abilities/Traits:
        Earth Maiden Path: Increased stamina regeneration in natural environments (+10%).
        Moon Queen Path: Enhanced critical hit chance when fighting under open skies or moonlight (+5%).

    Scaling and Progression:
        Spells scale with levels and unlock new abilities based on progression and path choices.
        Advanced stages of her abilities introduce synergies between Earth and Moon elements.

    Environmental Interactions:
        Earth Maiden Path: Spells gain a 10% power boost in natural settings like forests or caves.
        Moon Queen Path: Spells are 15% stronger during nighttime or under an open sky.

Summary

Kiến Thi Thuý Anh’s dual-path system provides dynamic gameplay options, reflecting her dual heritage. Whether players choose the Earth Maiden path for resilience and healing or the Moon Queen path for celestial power and tactical dominance, her abilities offer flexibility, thematic depth, and synergistic potential with other party members.

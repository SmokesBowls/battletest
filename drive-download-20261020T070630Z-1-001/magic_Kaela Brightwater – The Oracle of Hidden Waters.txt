4. Kaela Brightwater – The Oracle of Hidden Waters

Role: Diviner and Seer, Specialist in Water Magic and Prophecy

Background: Kaela is a mystic who has lived in hiding by Earth’s coasts and rivers, practicing water divination and communicating with water spirits. She is believed to be able to see through reflections and can even gaze into the future, though she only shares her visions sparingly.

Influence: Kaela’s divination and water magic provide the society with a prophetic edge, offering insights into future events and hidden truths. She often assists other practitioners by providing forewarnings and wisdom, though her cryptic nature makes her advice both invaluable and frustrating.

Potential Impact: Kaela’s presence creates a faction within the society that focuses on scrying, prophecy, and water manipulation, establishing a source of foresight and guidance for the group. However, her visions could lead to ethical dilemmas as she reveals possible futures, which could conflict with the ambitions of members like Jarek and Nyssa.

Personality: Calm, mystical, and distant, Kaela is known for her cryptic speech and tendency to speak in riddles. She is respected but also feared, as her prophecies are rarely comforting.

3. Nyssa the Untethered and Kaela Brightwater:

Potential Dynamics: Nyssa and Kaela’s interests overlap in prophecy and cosmic timelines, but Kaela’s water-based scrying and divination bring a different flavor to her abilities. Nyssa’s logical approach to time might conflict with Kaela’s intuitive, cryptic prophecies, sparking intellectual rivalry and respect.

Potential Alliance: Together, they might work to document and preserve timelines, forming a subsect of scholars focused on recording and interpreting history and possible futures.

3. Thaddeus Blackwood – The Arcane Alchemist

Role: Specialist in Arcane Science, Transmutation, and Alchemical Potions

Background: Thaddeus is an eccentric alchemist who’s been secretly experimenting with magical compounds and transmutation. He hides his work under the guise of an apothecary, blending potions for both mundane and magical ailments, but his true passion lies in understanding the raw forces of magic through alchemical reactions.

Influence: Thaddeus offers practical magical knowledge, teaching society members how to manipulate matter and energy through alchemy. His lab is stocked with rare materials, and his work explores the intersection between science and magic, blending the mystical and the tangible in unique ways.

Potential Impact: Thaddeus’ focus on practical, transformative magic would be a balancing force within the society, emphasizing magic as a tool rather than a cosmic mystery. His methods could lead to internal conflicts with those who view magic as sacred or inherently bound to certain rules.

Personality: Quirky, intense, and often scattered, Thaddeus is both brilliant and unconventional. His curiosity drives him, and he’s quick to test theories without always thinking through the consequences.

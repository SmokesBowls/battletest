# Psionic Fracture

**Psionic Fracture** is the term given to an emergent anomaly first observed in Geralt. Unlike other humans, who require glyphs, focus rituals, or cybernetic augmentation to interact with vril, Geralt does so **instinctively**. His control is erratic, emotional, and raw—but undeniably potent.

This ability bypasses traditional magic systems, drawing the attention of both **the Nefereti** and **Applicators**, who detect a frequency anomaly within him that **should not exist**.

Whether a result of genetic corruption, ancient soul imprinting, or something older still, the Psionic Fracture grants players:

- **Telekinetic Grasp** (mid-range object manipulation)  
- **Vril Echo Pulse** (stun/disrupt effect based on emotional state)  
- Later unlocks may include **Vessel Awakening** or **Tiamat’s Core** (TBD)

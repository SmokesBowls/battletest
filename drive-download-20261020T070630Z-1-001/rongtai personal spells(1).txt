Core Spells (3-5)
Shadow Nova

    Effect: Releases a pulse of shadow energy, dealing 75% of Shadow’s base damage to all nearby enemies and applying Weakened Shadows (-15% attack power) for 2 turns.
    Element: Shadow
    Visual/Auditory Cue: A dark wave ripples outward, accompanied by a low, ominous hum.

Ethereal Chains

    Effect: Binds enemies with shadowy tendrils, reducing their movement speed by 30% and dealing 10% DoT for 3 turns.
    Element: Shadow + Spirit
    Visual/Auditory Cue: Chains of shadow extend from the ground, accompanied by ghostly whispers.

Umbral Shield

    Effect: Surrounds an ally with a barrier that absorbs damage equal to 20% of Rongtai’s max HP. Has a 25% chance to reflect 50% of absorbed damage back to attackers.
    Element: Shadow
    Visual/Auditory Cue: A swirling black barrier envelops the ally, with a faint metallic ringing sound upon activation.

Supportive/Utility Spells (2-4)
Veil of Shadows

    Effect: Creates a shadowy haze that confuses enemies, giving them a 50% chance to miss their attacks or target their allies for 2 turns.
    Element: Shadow
    Visual/Auditory Cue: A dense fog of darkness spreads, muffling sounds and distorting visibility.

Twilight Barrier

    Effect: Summons a shadow wall that blocks projectiles and slows enemies passing through by 20% for 3 turns.
    Element: Shadow + Earth
    Visual/Auditory Cue: A dark, translucent wall rises from the ground, crackling with energy.

Ultimate/Power Spells (1-2)
Shadow Blast (Earned through trials)

    Effect: An explosion of blackness consumes all enemies within range, displacing them into the ethereal realm for 2 turns, removing them from combat temporarily.
    Element: Shadow + Spirit
    Visual/Auditory Cue: A massive surge of darkness erupts, accompanied by a deafening, ethereal roar.

Ethereal Resonance (If Shadow Blast is not earned)

    Effect: Amplifies the effects of all her spells, increasing their damage and utility by 25% for 3 turns.
    Element: Spirit
    Visual/Auditory Cue: A pulsating aura of shadow surrounds Rongtai, accompanied by a deep, resonant hum.

Situational Spells (1-3)
Shadow Reap

    Effect: Absorbs the life force of enemies in a small radius, healing allies for 15% of their max HP and reducing enemy attack power by 10% for 2 turns.
    Element: Shadow
    Visual/Auditory Cue: A swirling vortex of shadows drains energy from enemies, with a faint, echoing wail.

Phantom Mirror

    Effect: Creates a shadow clone of Rongtai that distracts enemies for 2 turns. The clone has 25% of Rongtai’s max HP and can trigger traps or reflect 50% of spells back to attackers.
    Element: Shadow
    Visual/Auditory Cue: A dark silhouette of Rongtai materializes, with a faint shimmer and ghostly echoes.

Dark Veil

    Effect: Grants stealth to herself or an ally for 2 turns, increasing movement speed by 20% and evasion by 15% during this period.
    Element: Shadow
    Visual/Auditory Cue: A cascading veil of darkness cloaks the target, accompanied by a soft, ethereal hum.

Resource System

    Shadow Essence: Spells consume shadow essence, a unique resource replenished through basic attacks or absorbing enemy energy via spells like Shadow Reap.

Synergy with Ethereal Mechanics

    Spells like Shadow Blast and Phantom Mirror create unique opportunities to exploit the ethereal realm, providing strategic depth.

Narrative Integration

Rongtai’s abilities are tied to her Asuren heritage, with each spell representing her mastery of survival and her role as a bridge between realms.

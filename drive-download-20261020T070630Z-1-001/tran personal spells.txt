Core Spells (3-5)
Crimson Flames

    Effect: A basic fire attack dealing 100% of Fire’s base damage with a 10% Corrosive Burn DoT for 2 turns. Effective against armor.
    Element: Fire
    Visual/Auditory Cue: Intense crimson flames erupt, crackling with a sharp hiss.

Blood Infusion

    Effect: Buffs an ally, granting +20% attack or +15% defense for 3 turns. Costs 10% of Trần’s current HP.
    Element: Spirit + Fire
    Visual/Auditory Cue: A glowing red aura envelops the ally, pulsing with vitality.

Dragon’s Roar

    Effect: Deals AoE damage (75% of Fire’s base damage) and reduces enemy defense by 15% for 2 turns.
    Element: Fire
    Visual/Auditory Cue: A deafening roar shakes the battlefield, accompanied by fiery shockwaves.

Supportive/Utility Spells (2-4)
Blood Shield

    Effect: Creates a barrier absorbing damage equal to 20% of Trần’s max HP. Reflects 15% of absorbed damage back to melee attackers.
    Element: Spirit
    Visual/Auditory Cue: A translucent crimson shield forms, with a faint heartbeat sound.

Elemental Resonance

    Effect: Increases elemental attack effectiveness for the party by 10% for 3 turns. Amplifies effects of elemental combos.
    Element: Fire + Spirit
    Visual/Auditory Cue: A radiant glow pulses outward from Trần, amplifying elemental energy.

Dragon’s Insight

    Effect: Reveals hidden enemies for 2 turns and grants allies a 10% evasion boost or temporary invulnerability for 1 turn.
    Element: Spirit
    Visual/Auditory Cue: Trần’s eyes glow brightly, with a faint hum of focused energy.

Ultimate/Power Spells (1-2)
Dragon Ascension

    Effect: Transforms Trần into his dragon form for 3 turns, granting +25% attack, +20% defense, and enhanced abilities.

    Visual/Auditory Cue: Flames and light engulf Trần as his dragon form emerges, accompanied by a resounding roar.

Crimson Cataclysm

    Effect: A massive AoE attack dealing 150% of Fire’s base damage, reducing enemy defense by 20%, and applying Burning (10% DoT for 3 turns).

    Visual/Auditory Cue: A devastating inferno engulfs the battlefield, with a thunderous explosion.

Situational Spells (1-3)

    Visual/Auditory Cue: Streams of molten fire shoot forward, hissing as they corrode targets.

Blood Bond

    Effect: Links Trần’s health with an ally’s, sharing 50% of damage received or healing both for 10% HP over 2 turns.
    Element: Spirit
    Visual/Auditory Cue: A glowing tether of blood-red energy connects Trần and his ally.

Dragon’s Tail Sweep

    Effect: A melee AoE attack dealing 75% of Fire’s base damage, knocking back enemies and stunning them for 1 turn.
    Element: Fire + Physical
    Visual/Auditory Cue: A fiery tail sweeps across the battlefield, with a loud, explosive crack.

Summary

Trần An Tôn Giảng’s spell set balances offense, support, and utility, reflecting his destiny as a prophesied warrior. His Dragon Scale Armor evolves with him, unlocking new abilities and combat options. The Queen’s Guard adds a layer of strategic depth, emphasizing his role as a protector. These abilities are designed to synergize with other party members while showcasing his thematic role as the Crimson Blood Dragon.

WATER:

Primary Stats per Level:
```
Level 1:
* Basic Attack: Single water burst (75 damage)
* AoE: Adjacent pads (25 splash damage)
* MP Cost: 25

Level 2:
* Enhanced Attack: Dual water bursts (2x65 damage)
* Expanded AoE: Two pad radius (35 splash)
* MP Cost: 45

Level 3:
* Ultimate: Tidal Wave (250 damage)
* Full battlefield AoE (75 splash)
* MP Cost: 75
```

Element Interactions (scaling with levels):
```
vs Fire:
* Level 1: Cancel + Steam (25 DoT, 3 turns)
* Level 2: Steam (35 DoT, 3 turns)
* Level 3: Steam (50 DoT, 4 turns)

vs Lightning:
* Level 1: +50% Lightning damage, Wet status (2 turns)
* Level 2: +75% Lightning damage, Wet status (3 turns)
* Level 3: +100% Lightning damage, Wet status (4 turns)

vs Earth:
* Level 1: Mud slow (25% movement), Ground saturation
* Level 2: Mud slow (35%), Extended saturation
* Level 3: Mud slow (50%), Full field saturation

vs Ice:

* Level 2: Freeze chance +35%, Slick surface (2 pads)
* Level 3: Freeze chance +50%, Full field ice effect

vs Shadow:
* Level 1: Reveal hidden (1 turn), Dark water (25 bonus damage)
* Level 2: Reveal hidden (2 turns), Dark water (35 bonus damage)
* Level 3: Reveal hidden (3 turns), Dark water (50 bonus damage)

vs Spirit:
* Level 1: Purify (removes 1 debuff), Heal 25 HP
* Level 2: Purify (removes 2 debuffs), Heal 35 HP
* Level 3: Purify (removes all debuffs), Heal 50 HP

vs Moon:
* Level 1: Tidal boost (+25% damage), Low gravity (1 turn)
```
